How it works

App installed: Android can open Alexa Tap directly through a verified HTTPS App Link. The app sends the saved track details and finishes.

App not installed: the same link opens this page, where the APK can be downloaded.

Track details come from Android media notifications. Some apps or devices may not expose track details.
